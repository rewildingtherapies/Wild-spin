The Outdoor PracticeWild Spin

How to play

Press SPIN. The wheel picks one of six categories and shows a challenge card. Read it out, enjoy it together, then spin again.

Three ways to play

  • Free Play. No scores and no clock. Just spin and chat.
  • Together. Everyone is one big team. Each finished challenge adds a leaf to the pile.
  • Two Teams. Split the room in two and rename the teams. Teams take turns. Use the + and − buttons to award points if you want them. Scoring is optional.

On each card

  • Reveal answer shows the answer for quiz questions and teasers.
  • Another one gives a new card from the same category.
  • Skip this one puts the card back in the pile and shows a different one. Use it freely.
  • Spin again picks a new category.

Facilitator notes

  • Animal Moves are made for standing and moving, with a sitting-down version on every card. Clear a little space first and check the floor is safe.
  • Anyone can pass on any card. Encourage quieter children without pushing them.
  • Cards do not repeat until every card in that category has been used.
  • Progress saves on this computer. Press New session to start fresh.
  • Calm motion makes the wheel turn gently without fast spinning.
